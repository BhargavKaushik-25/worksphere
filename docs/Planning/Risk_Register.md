# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Risk Register for the WorkSphere project.

It provides a structured approach for identifying, documenting,
assessing, prioritizing, monitoring, and mitigating project risks
throughout the software development lifecycle.

The objective is to maintain visibility into potential risks and enable
timely actions that minimize their impact on project scope, schedule,
cost, quality, and business objectives.

This document serves as the primary reference for:

- Project Managers
- Product Owners
- Technical Leads
- Software Architects
- Development Team
- QA Engineers

---

# 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Risk identification
- Risk assessment
- Risk categorization
- Risk prioritization
- Risk ownership
- Mitigation planning
- Risk monitoring
- Risk reporting

---

# 3. Risk Management Objectives

The Risk Register is designed to achieve:

- Early identification of project risks
- Consistent risk assessment
- Effective mitigation planning
- Improved decision-making
- Reduced project uncertainty
- Better stakeholder visibility
- Increased delivery confidence
- Continuous risk monitoring
- Improved project governance
- Successful project delivery

# 4. Risk Management Principles

The following principles govern risk management throughout the
WorkSphere project.

+-----------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| Principle | Description                                                   |
+-----------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| RISK-001  | Risks shall be identified as early as possible.               |
| RISK-002  | Every significant risk shall have an assigned owner.          |
| RISK-003  | Risks shall be assessed consistently.                         |
| RISK-004  | High-priority risks shall receive mitigation plans.           |
| RISK-005  | Risk status shall be reviewed regularly.                      |
| RISK-006  | Risk decisions shall be documented.                           |
| RISK-007  | Risks shall remain traceable throughout the project.          |
| RISK-008  | Emerging risks shall be added promptly.                       |
| RISK-009  | Stakeholders shall be informed of major risks.                |
| RISK-010  | Risk management is a continuous project activity.             |
+-----------+---------------------------------------------------------------+

# 5. Risk Categories

## Overview

Project risks are grouped into categories to simplify identification,
assessment, reporting, and mitigation.

Categorization helps ensure balanced attention across technical,
business, operational, and organizational concerns.

---

## Risk Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Technical | Architecture, implementation, integration, and technology risks |
| Business | Requirement changes, stakeholder expectations, and funding |
| Schedule | Delays, estimation errors, and milestone slippage |
| Resource | Skill shortages, availability, and team capacity |
| Security | Vulnerabilities, unauthorized access, and compliance issues |
| Infrastructure | Cloud services, networking, databases, and environments |
| Operational | Deployment, monitoring, support, and maintenance risks |
| External | Third-party services, vendors, regulations, and dependencies |

---

## Category Standards

| Standard ID | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| CAT-001 | Every identified risk shall belong to at least one category. |
| CAT-002 | Risk categories shall be reviewed periodically. |
| CAT-003 | Cross-functional risks may belong to multiple categories. |
| CAT-004 | Category ownership shall be clearly defined. |
| CAT-005 | Category trends shall be monitored over time. |

---

# 6. Risk Assessment Matrix

## Overview

Each identified risk shall be evaluated based on its probability of
occurrence and its potential impact on the project.

The combination of probability and impact determines the overall risk
rating.

---

## Probability Levels

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Very Low | Rare occurrence |
| Low | Unlikely |
| Medium | Possible |
| High | Likely |
| Very High | Almost certain |

---

## Impact Levels

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Very Low | Negligible impact |
| Low | Minor project impact |
| Medium | Noticeable impact requiring management |
| High | Significant impact on project objectives |
| Critical | Severe impact on delivery or business operations |

---

## Risk Matrix

| Probability \ Impact | Very Low | Low | Medium | High | Critical |
|----------------------|----------|-----|--------|------|----------|
| Very High | Medium | High | High | Critical | Critical |
| High | Medium | Medium | High | Critical | Critical |
| Medium | Low | Medium | Medium | High | Critical |
| Low | Low | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| Very Low | Low | Low | Low | Medium | Medium |

---

## Assessment Standards

| Standard ID | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| ASSESS-001 | Probability and impact shall be evaluated independently. |
| ASSESS-002 | Assessment shall be based on available evidence. |
| ASSESS-003 | High and Critical risks shall be reviewed by project leadership. |
| ASSESS-004 | Risk ratings shall be updated when circumstances change. |
| ASSESS-005 | Assessment assumptions shall be documented where necessary. |

# 8. Risk Response Strategy

## Overview

Each identified risk shall have a planned response strategy based on its
probability, impact, and overall priority.

The selected response shall minimize the likelihood of occurrence or
reduce the resulting impact on the project.

---

## Risk Response Types

| Strategy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Avoid | Eliminate the risk by changing the project approach |
| Mitigate | Reduce the probability or impact of the risk |
| Transfer | Shift responsibility to a third party where appropriate |
| Accept | Acknowledge the risk and monitor it without immediate action |

---

## Response Standards

| Standard ID | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| RESP-001 | Every High and Critical risk shall have a documented response plan. |
| RESP-002 | Risk responses shall include actionable mitigation activities. |
| RESP-003 | Risk owners shall monitor mitigation progress. |
| RESP-004 | Response effectiveness shall be reviewed periodically. |
| RESP-005 | Escalated risks shall be communicated to stakeholders. |

---

# 9. Risk Monitoring

## Overview

Project risks shall be monitored continuously throughout the project
lifecycle to ensure timely identification of changes and effective
implementation of mitigation plans.

---

## Monitoring Activities

| Activity | Frequency |
|----------|-----------|
| Risk Register Review | Weekly |
| Sprint Risk Review | Every Sprint |
| Milestone Risk Review | At Major Milestones |
| Architecture Risk Review | As Required |
| Release Risk Review | Before Every Production Release |

---

## Monitoring Standards

| Standard ID | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| MONITOR-001 | Risk status shall be reviewed regularly. |
| MONITOR-002 | New risks shall be added immediately after identification. |
| MONITOR-003 | Closed risks shall remain documented for historical reference. |
| MONITOR-004 | Ineffective mitigation plans shall be revised. |
| MONITOR-005 | Risk reports shall be available to project stakeholders. |

# 11. Risk Review and Escalation

## Overview

Risk reviews ensure that identified risks remain current, mitigation
activities are progressing as planned, and emerging risks are captured
promptly.

Escalation shall occur whenever a risk exceeds the authority or
capability of the assigned owner.

---

## Risk Review Standards

| Standard ID | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| REVIEW-001 | Risk reviews shall be conducted on the defined schedule. |
| REVIEW-002 | High and Critical risks shall be reviewed by project leadership. |
| REVIEW-003 | Mitigation effectiveness shall be evaluated regularly. |
| REVIEW-004 | Escalated risks shall include documented justification. |
| REVIEW-005 | Review outcomes shall be recorded in the Risk Register. |

# 12. Continuous Improvement

The WorkSphere project shall continuously improve its risk management
process through periodic reviews, retrospectives, and lessons learned.

Continuous improvement activities include:

- Reviewing recurring project risks
- Improving mitigation strategies
- Updating risk assessment criteria
- Refining project planning practices
- Strengthening stakeholder communication
- Improving monitoring and reporting
- Sharing lessons learned across the team
- Updating project documentation
